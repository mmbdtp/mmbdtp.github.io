## Data

We have an Illumina dataset (three paired-end samples) from a human gut virome study.
We also have a Nanopore sequencing of a bacterial isolate.

:bulb: Inside `week2` find these datasets

## What we will do

1. Understand the FASTQ format
2. Identify some criteria for Quality Control of sequencing outputs
3. Perform a quality profiling of the datasets
4. Use kraken to identify contamination in the datasets
5. Understand the SAM/BAM format, and familiarise with a [mapping experiment](https://corebio.info/ebame-viromics/modules/extras/map/).
